# Privacy Policy

**LAN Media Streaming (LAN Media Sender and LAN Media Receiver)**

**Effective date:** September 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **LAN Media Sender** and **LAN Media Receiver**
applications ("the apps"), which are part of the open-source LAN Media Streaming
project. The apps are developed and maintained by Mike Young ("we," "us").

## Summary

**We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or user data.**
The apps have no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting,
and make no connection to the internet or to any server operated by us or by any
third party. Everything the apps do happens on your own local network.

## What the apps do

LAN Media Sender captures a device's screen — and, optionally, its audio — and
streams it over your local area network (LAN) to a LAN Media Receiver that you
choose. LAN Media Receiver displays such a stream. The screen and audio stream is
sent directly, device to device, over your local network. It is not recorded, not
stored, and is never sent to us or to any third party. We have no access to it.

## Data collection

We do not collect any data. Specifically, the apps do not collect or transmit any
of the following: location, name, email address, contacts, messages, photos or
videos from your library, files or documents, calendar data, financial or health
information, device identifiers or advertising IDs, app-usage or analytics data,
diagnostics, or crash logs.

## Information stored on your device

The apps save your own configuration on your device only — for example, the
receiver name, address, port, password, video settings, and the pinned security
certificate fingerprint. This information is stored locally in the app's private
storage on your device, is used solely to operate the app, and is never
transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions

The apps request only the permissions needed to function, and use them only for
that purpose:

- **Screen capture (MediaProjection):** to capture the screen you choose to
  stream. Android requires your explicit on-screen consent each time capture
  begins.
- **Record audio (used for playback/system audio capture):** to include device
  audio in the stream, only when you enable that option. It is used to capture
  playback audio for streaming, not to make private recordings, and the audio is
  never stored or sent to us.
- **Network access / local network:** to send or receive the stream and to
  discover devices on your local network. No internet connection is made.
- **Foreground service / notifications:** to keep streaming or receiving running
  reliably and to show its status.

## Security

Connections between the sender and receiver can be encrypted using TLS, with
trust-on-first-use certificate pinning, and may be protected with a password that
you set. Because all traffic stays on your local network and never reaches us,
you remain in control of it.

## Children's privacy

The apps were designed with educational (including K-12) use in mind. Because the
apps collect no personal information from anyone, they collect no personal
information from children.

## Third-party services

The apps use no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.
They incorporate open-source software libraries (such as AndroidX and Material
Components) that run entirely on the device and do not transmit your data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location
with a revised effective date.
